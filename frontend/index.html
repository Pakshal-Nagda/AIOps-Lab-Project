Project Medhā — User Manual

Welcome to Project Medhā!

Project Medhā is an intelligent Sanskrit & Prakrit recitation assistant that helps students and practitioners master pronunciation, rhythm, and memorization of sacred verses.

1 Input Your Verse

Paste any Sanskrit or Prakrit shloka or sutra into the top input box. It can be single-line or multi-line (1 to 100+ lines). Spaces and typographic punctuation are purely visual conventions and will never penalize your recitation score.

2 Blind Memorization Practice (Optional)

Click the "Hide Text for Blind Memorization Test" button. The reference text will be hidden so you can test your memory. As you recite, verified lines will materialize onto the canvas below in real-time!

3 Start Reciting

Press "Begin Recitation" and allow microphone access. Speak naturally. You do not need to wait for a line to appear before starting the next line—reciting with unbroken breath across verse boundaries is fully supported!

4 Understanding Feedback

  • Green letters: Pronounced accurately.
  • Red underline: Pronunciation error. Hover or click to see what was expected vs heard.
  • Yellow underline: Skipped or omitted syllable.
  • [⏸️ 1.8s]: Unusually long hesitation (>1.5 seconds) detected by the Voice Activity Detector.
